## 0. Mid-run integrity check (rex, F31) and a mid-run environment failure (this task)

**Rex's arm-selection question, answered:** `assign_arm=arm` is passed explicitly on
every single invocation (`child_solve.py`: `api.solve(prob_info, timelimit,
assign_arm=arm)`), `arm` comes straight from argv and is never `None`, so
`api._resolve_arm`'s environment-variable fallback is structurally unreachable here.

check landed onward; every audited row shows `assign_arm_resolved == requested_arm`
(`match=True`). Rows before the audit-trail existed are not independently suspect:
same source-verified mechanism, plus the empirical pattern (arms produce different,
reproducible, instance-dependent objectives on every gate instance) rules out a
silent env-tie, which would force byte-identical rows across arms.

## 2. t=300 spot axis — prob_31 (complete) and prob_38 (blocked mid-run, §0)

### prob_31 @ t=300, N=3, both arms — clean, complete, feasible 6/6

| arm | obj1 | objective | wall (s) | margin vs 270s | full pass ran? | LB gap |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| baseline   | 941 (×3, identical) | 15,020,801 | 163–205 | +65..+107 | **Yes** | "certified", gap=941 |
| congestion | 947 (×3, identical) | 15,301,292 | 234–241 | +29..+36  | **Yes** | "certified", gap=947 |

**Mechanism confirmed exactly as predicted:** at t=300 the full pass runs for both
arms (vs budget-skipped at t=60), and **the direction reverses** — baseline now
**wins** (941 vs 947 obj1, 15.02M vs 15.30M objective, ~1.9% better), the opposite
of the t=60 result on the same instance (congestion won by 56% there). This is not
noise — both arms are perfectly deterministic across all 3 reps.

**LB-gap caveat, stated because it would otherwise look like a strong certificate:**
`lb_gap` here equals `obj1` exactly for both arms (941=941, 947=947), which means the
*computed* lower bound itself is **0** — `solver.bounds._cumulative_lb`'s own
documented fallback ("UNKNOWN etc.: 0 is always a sound lower bound") on a CP-SAT
call that hit its 2s time cap without converging. `lb_gap_note="certified"` is
technically correct (it IS a certified, if trivial, bound) but this is **not** a tight
proof — flagging so nobody reads "certified" as "near-optimal."

## 4. A small bug I found and fixed in my own harness (not solver code)

Early in the panel, my LB-gap derivation defaulted `delayed = p.get("delayed", {})`
when the `full` pass entry was **skipped** (no `delayed` key at all in that case) and
then read the empty default as "zero delayed bays" → fabricated
`lb_gap_note="proof(zero-tardy)"` even when `obj1` was clearly nonzero (two prob_21
rows, reps 1–2, before the fix). Fixed to gate on `full_ran` explicitly; all
subsequent rows correctly show `"n/a(full skipped: ...)"` instead. The two
pre-fix rows' feasibility/objective/wall values are unaffected (only the label was
wrong) and are kept in the CSV with the corrected reading noted here rather than
silently rewritten.

## 6. Verdict

**INCONCLUSIVE — blocked, not REJECT and not PROMOTE.**

Reasons, explicit:

- The gate panel (t=60, N=5, the one leg that ran clean end-to-end) is genuinely
  **mixed**: 4 real wins, 1 real regression (prob_21), 1 inconclusive-within-noise
  (prob_38), 0 infeasible, 0 timeout, 0 margin breach on that leg alone.
- The t=300 spot axis — required specifically to test whether the t=60 win survives
  into the regime where the full pass actually runs — is **incomplete** (prob_38: 1/6
  valid) and the **one instance that did complete cleanly (prob_31) shows the win
  reversing**, which is a direct hit against a PROMOTE call based on t=60 data alone.
- The screening sweep (34 instances) **never started** — no evidence either way on
  the bulk of the training set.
- A mid-run, uncommitted deletion of `utils.py`/`myalgorithm.py`/`baseline_greedy.py`/
  `legacy_entry.py` from `ogc2026/baseline/` (tree still in that state at stamp time)
  makes the environment **not currently runnable** for this harness or, as far as
  this measurement can tell, for the real entry point either.
- Per the "local-only, not gauntlet-valid" stamp (macOS, 8 cores, no isolation, and
  not quiet by this task's own pre-check), **nothing here can make anything
  submit-eligible regardless of outcome** — consistent with the task's own framing.

**What would resolve this:** (a) confirm the tree is stable (either `utils.py` etc.
restored to `ogc2026/baseline/` or a new canonical path communicated), (b) re-run
the prob_38 t=300 leg (6 reps) and complete the 34-instance screening sweep, (c) on
promote consideration, weight the t=300 reversal on prob_31 heavily — it is the only
direct evidence of what happens when the "mass-tail budget-skip" premise the arm was
built for no longer holds, and it points away from the arm, not toward it.
